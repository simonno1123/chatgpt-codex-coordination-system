BOOTSTRAP AUTHORITY CONSTRAINTS:
ACCEPTED AS DESIGN REQUIREMENTS / NOT IMPLEMENTED

Bootstrap Authority may be designed only to establish the minimum Initial
Governance State through separately governed procedures. It must not become
General Governance Authority.

an unlimited authorization path, bypass Contract or Constitutional constraints,
or ratify or activate itself.

consumption, and latest termination conditions.

Bootstrap Authority must terminate after successful authority transfer into an
activated Initial Governance State or after rejection, expiry, revocation,
failed Ratification, or a material defect.
