## 3. Authorization Model Comparison (Model A vs B vs C)

### Model A: Role-Based Standing Authorization
- **Assessment**: Simple with minimal process overhead.
- **Risk**: Role labels can be spoofed or misinterpreted as unconstrained authority across all targets. Target binding is weak.

### Model B: Per-Review Decision Authorization
- **Assessment**: Maximum target and scope confinement.
- **Risk**: High operational friction and severe risk of **Recursive Authorization** (requiring an authorization for the authorization, ad infinitum).

### Model C: Hybrid Authorization (Recommended Baseline)
- **Concept**:
  ```text
  Standing Role Permission + Target SHA-256 Digest Grant = Governed Review
  ```
- **Assessment**: Combines reusable standing role capabilities (`EXTERNAL_ADVISORY` -> `advisory_generate`) with target-specific digest binding (`GP-003` SHA-256 digest).
- **Advisory Finding**: Model C provides the optimal non-recursive, highly traceable architecture for ACOS governance reviews.
